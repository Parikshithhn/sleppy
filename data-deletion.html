Sleppy Esports User Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their personal information associated with the Sleppy Esports WhatsApp service.

How to request deletion

Contact the Sleppy Esports administrator and provide the WhatsApp number or player information associated with the account.

The administrator will review the request and delete eligible personal data where retention is not required for legitimate team, competition, security or legal purposes.

For assistance with a deletion request, contact the Sleppy Esports administrator.
