Sleppy Esports Privacy Policy

Sleppy Esports uses this service to manage its esports team through WhatsApp. Information may include player name, IGN, BGMI UID, WhatsApp number, roster information, attendance, leave records and esports performance records.

Information is used for team management, scheduling, attendance, leave management, match records, notifications and related esports operations.

We do not ask users to provide passwords, payment-card information or unrelated sensitive information through this service.

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Sleppy Esports administrator.
